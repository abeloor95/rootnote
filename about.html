About Rootnote

Rootnote is an open platform where independent artists, managers, and anyone working in music can search through academic-level insightful articles delving deep into the industry, past and present. Whether you're revisiting the Billboard charts of the 1960s or trying to understand where AI-generated music and usage rights are headed, Rootnote is built to hold that information in one place and make it easily accessible to those who search for it. The goal is straightforward: promote real discussion and educate anyone genuinely curious about the logistics, foundations, and cultural shifts that built the music industry into what it is today. A place where insightful conversation can happen, and education stays at the center of making every musician's artistry better backed by scholar-level information.

What Rootnote is

What you'll find here

Chart history, contract and rights breakdowns, sampling law, production trends, and the cultural shifts behind them, covered with the same rigor you'd expect from a course, not a hot take.

Who it's for

Independent artists, managers, and anyone working in the industry who wants grounded, sourced information instead of recycled takes, alongside anyone simply curious about how music actually gets made and monetized.

How we work

Rootnote is built as an open platform, not a closed publication. The goal is to promote discussion and put education first, so the site grows as much through conversation as it does through new lessons and articles.
